# YouTube release and monetization-readiness scope

Policy review date: 2026-08-24

Official references:

- [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- [Disclosing use of GenAI content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)

Policies change. A release owner must re-read the official pages and update the
review date before relying on this document for a release.

## What the current policy means for Melotrail

YouTube evaluates monetization at channel and content level. The official
monetization policy emphasizes original, authentic content and identifies
generic, repetitive, mass-produced, or template-like output as inauthentic.
Rights, copyright, Community Guidelines, advertiser-friendly rules, and other
program policies also apply.

YouTube's GenAI disclosure guidance explicitly includes AI-generated music as
an example requiring disclosure. The guidance also states that disclosure does
not itself reduce audience or monetization eligibility. Disclosure is therefore
a transparency requirement, not proof that a release is or is not monetizable.

Melotrail must not display “YouTube approved,” “guaranteed monetizable,” or an
equivalent claim.

## Product responsibility

Melotrail can help a creator produce and document:

- an original musician-supplied melody and authoritative harmony/structure;
- substantial human decisions and approvals throughout the pipeline;
- recognizable motif and melody lineage;
- arrangement and release-to-release similarity warnings;
- source, instrument, sample, model, and output provenance;
- required attribution text;
- whether selected generative stages make AI-use disclosure appropriate;
- objective composition/audio findings and recorded listening approval;
- a YouTube release metadata draft for human review.

Melotrail cannot determine:

- ownership or copyrightability in a jurisdiction;
- whether a source infringes another work or will receive a Content ID claim;
- whether a channel as a whole is sufficiently original/authentic;
- Community Guidelines or advertiser suitability for accompanying visuals,
  lyrics, title, thumbnail, description, or channel history;
- Partner Program thresholds, review outcomes, or future policy decisions;
- commercial success, views, retention, or revenue.

## Commercial-ready evidence gate

A release may be labeled `COMMERCIAL_EVIDENCE_READY` only when:

1. Every source has a current rights attestation and immutable hash.
2. Every used instrument/sample/model dependency has an admitted commercial
   license or explicit reviewed ownership record.
3. Required attribution is complete and generated from the frozen used-stem
   lineage.
4. The approved canonical melody retains its confirmed signature motif and has
   no unresolved musical blockers.
5. Arrangement, mix, master, and export hashes close over the exact selected
   lineage.
6. AI-use metadata reflects actual selected generative stages.
7. Cross-release similarity is reviewed when comparison releases exist; a high
   score is advisory and requires a human originality decision.
8. The policy review date is current under the repository's release policy.
9. A human confirms release title, description, visuals, lyrics, rights,
   disclosure, and channel context outside Melotrail.

`COMMERCIAL_EVIDENCE_READY` means evidence is complete under Melotrail's current
policy. It is not legal advice, platform approval, or a monetization guarantee.

## Anti-template product requirements

The composition pipeline should reduce accidental generic output without
inventing a fake monetization score:

- preserve creator melody, harmony, structure, motif, and overrides;
- require meaningful section contrast rather than identical density everywhere;
- prevent schema examples and default prompts from becoming repeated musical
  templates;
- store arrangement/groove/orchestration fingerprints for optional comparison;
- warn when multiple releases are unusually interchangeable;
- keep re-plan scope bounded to arrangement, groove, or orchestration unless the
  musician explicitly edits melody/harmony/structure;
- require human listening and originality signoff for a release.

The application must never optimize for evading detection or platform review.
